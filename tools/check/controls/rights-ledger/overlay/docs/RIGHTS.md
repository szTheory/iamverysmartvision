# Rights policy (control tree)

This is the control tree's copy of the operator-facing rights page.
The `manifests` step requires one row here for every ledger row in `data/rights/`, keyed on the row's `item` phrase.

## Questions this milestone touches

| Item | Disposition | What that means |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| a toy owner image | `owner-local-only` | may be named; the bytes stay with the owner |
| a toy reference document | `cleared` | may be named; nothing is vendored |
| a toy stray row | `owner-local-only` | may be named; planted here so its row is not the finding |
| a toy row with a blank taken cell | `owner-local-only` | may be named; its ledger row answers nothing, so it must be refused by name |
