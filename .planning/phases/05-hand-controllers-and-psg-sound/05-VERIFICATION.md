## Goal achievement

The goal is met in the sense the gate can test, and I confirmed it by running the code rather than by reading the earlier record. A guest reads each of 31 controls on two ports through `$01FE` and `$01FF`; input takes effect at CPU-unit starts and gives the same guest-visible state and digests however a run is sliced; the PSG model produces integer chip-rate samples and a cycle-stamped write log that are slicing-invariant, replay-complete and consistent with the CPU-side bus trace; all of it works through the public API, the runner process and the installed package (the consumer's `ctrl.digest.*` lines equal the runner golden's digests: cpu `d87bcb16...`, audio `49389f10...`, input `31ef9546...`, psglog `c98733eb...`). Reference PCM exists only in `tools/pcm` and the runner's operator-side `--audio-wav`: `nm -g` over `build/release/core/libivs_core.a` shows no `pcm`, `wav`, `resampl`, `dac` or `refpcm` symbol (0 matches), the install manifest holds the 10 files of the core package (archive, four headers, the exported CMake targets and config, the licence) and neither `tools/pcm` nor the runner.

What the goal does not include, and the record does not claim: that the byte table, the register map, the PSG rate chain, the noise register, the DAC levels or the electrical combination rule match a console. Those rest on tier 4 community sources, inference or labelled defaults, and are the handed-off items below.

## 3. Accepted caveats

- **Shared misreading.** The same author wrote the controller literal, the register literal, the derivations, the listings and the core. The blind second reads (controller 31 of 31 rows; PSG 90 of 120 vectors agree) are the same model family reading the same pages: a transcription cross-check, not independent evidence. Agreement between the pieces is not evidence about a console. This verifier is the same model family too: it re-ran the code and re-derived the table checks, but it did not read a console, a schematic or a data sheet.
- **Tier 4 pin table.** The controller table is a LOW-confidence tier 4 literal from two community sources that are likely one lineage (C16); there is no schematic. The second reads are done; none is pending. They do not raise a tier.
- **PSG page-reading verdict.** The 1980 and 1982 catalog pages of the AY-3-8910 do not print the closed-form frequency equations, the amplitude mode-bit sense or any DAC voltage label (`equations-printed: no`, `dac-labels-printed: no`). The earlier record said the vectors were "AY-8910 page equations by hand"; that wording overstated the sources and is corrected here: the printed material is the divide chains in words, the shape figures, the field widths and the port pull-ups, and the rate vectors are closed-form bounds on those chains. Every rule is `inference` at best (8910 data sheet applied to an AY-3-8914-class part, C11), 6 of 21 rules are `default-unknown` and 5 are `8914-community`.
- **DAC table wording.** The `psg.dac.levels` note of the profile calls levels 15 to 9 "the printed Fig. 9 labels". That figure is in the reprint that plan 05-01 could not obtain; the values come from a discuss-note reading and the cite already says they are not an independent source. The tag is `inference`, the core never reads the field, and the DAC vectors are policy rows, so no claim or raw-audio golden depends on it. The word "printed" is second-hand and should be softened the next time the profile is edited (a documentation fix, not a behaviour one).
- **Structural constants in the PSG engine.** Not profile fields, by design of the register-number-level model: three channels, the 4-bit level range and 16 envelope states per cycle (`IVS_PSG_ENV_STATES`, the 8910 table; the discrepancy log notes a 32-step variant in another part), the register field widths (12-bit tone period, 5-bit noise period, 16-bit envelope period, 4-bit fixed level), and the rule that select code 0 means the fixed level and codes 1 to 3 use the profile's shifts. A profile for a part with different widths or step counts would need engine changes. None of the items the success criterion names (period 0, envelope select, noise LFSR, DAC levels) is hard-coded.
- **C67 unit-start granularity is a contract.** An event takes effect at the first CPU-unit boundary whose start tick is at or after its tick; a real console may differ by up to one instruction. A refinement changes goldens that carry input.
- **Repeatability only.** The sample-exact goldens, the digests, the consumer golden and the `.refpcm` golden are self-recorded: they show the same machine produces the same bytes, not that the bytes are right. The `.refpcm` golden is labelled "reference conversion v1, converter repeatability only", is a simple aliasing reference and sits outside the raw-audio evidence path.
- **`BUFFER_TOO_SMALL` is reachable through the public API** for audio and the write log (`psg.buffers.too-small-no-write`, `psg.buffers.log-too-small`, `run.buffers.psglog-overlap`).
- **Composite digest version 2** and the golden re-blesses (four classified diffs: composite v2 in plan 05-03, audio live in 05-06, noise and envelope in 05-07, and the runner key list with the `psglog` domain in 05-09).
- **Counts against the D-25 guideline (A-08).** The guideline was about 365 test ids, at most 26 faults and about 22 controls; the phase now has 3778 expected test ids over both presets, 193 fault runs and 316 controls in the gate (Phase 5 added 956 ids, 42 faults and 39 controls before the review, plus 6 ids and 2 faults for the review fixes). The per-plan caps stayed binding; the guideline was exceeded.
- **Noise and envelope conventions are recorded, not measured:** noise first bits list the output after each shift, the envelope's first level change is 2 x EP steps after a restart (POL-PSG-02).
- **`psg.mix.truth-table` is polarity-invariant:** a flipped mixer sense does not fail that row; `PSG_MIXER_SENSE_INVERTED` is caught by `psg.mix.and-tone0` (registered under AUD-01).
- **Profile tag of `psg.mixer.port_dir_bits` (C73):** the field and the discrepancy both say `unknown-default-chosen`.
- **Review deferrals and carried residuals** (05-REVIEW.md, 05-SECURITY.md, status SECURED with 42 threats closed): IN-01 (port direction bits are read through the R7 read mask; both masks are `$00FF` for the one profile), IN-03 (`IVS_RUN_REQUEST_V1_SIZE` is 64 on every ABI), IN-05 (operator outputs created 0644), IN-06 (two unreachable `period == 0` branches); operator-path symlink trust of `--update-golden` and the content directory, watchdog orphan after a `SIGKILL` of the harness (test-only), no named control for the measured-tier claim rule (per 05-SECURITY.md the rule itself fires: a scratch tier edit makes the `claims` check fail; not re-run here), TOCTOU on the event array (documented), `refs-verified=3/4`, the two guarded test skips.
