## Goal achievement

The goal is met in the sense the gate can test. A guest reads each of 31 controls on two ports through the PSG port addresses `$01FE` and `$01FF`, input takes effect at CPU-unit starts and gives the same guest-visible state and digests however a run is sliced, and the PSG model produces integer chip-rate samples and a cycle-stamped write log that are slicing-invariant, replay-complete and consistent with the CPU-side bus trace, through the public API, the runner process and the installed package. Reference PCM exists only in `tools/pcm` and the runner's operator-side `--audio-wav`; the core and the installed prefix contain none (`pkg-install` inventory and public-symbol rule, control `core-symbols-pcm-defined`).

What the goal does not include, and the record does not claim: that the byte table, the register map, the PSG rate chain, the noise register, the DAC levels or the electrical combination rule match a console. Those rest on tier 4 community sources, inference or labelled defaults, and are the handed-off items below.

### Independent recomputation (executor, scratch directory outside the repository, not committed)

A short script re-derived the following from the committed literals and compared them with the `.expect`, the scenario and a real run of `diag-ctrl-psg-pass` (`--audio-raw` and `--psg-log-out` files of the operator options):

- Controller literal: all 31 rows have `byte == FF xor cleared`; a pin-to-bit map (pin 1 to `08`, 2 to `04`, 3 to `02`, 4 to `01`, 6 to `80`, 7 to `40`, 8 to `20`, 9 to `10`) fitted from the key rows and pin 9 from the side rows reproduces all 31 `cleared` masks (an internal-consistency check of the pins and cleared columns, not of the source).
- Poll sequences from the seven events and the literal masks: port 0 `FF 7E FF 5A FF FB 5B 1D`, port 1 `FF FD 9F FF`. Both equal the `.expect` words `0210..0217` and `0220..0223`.
- The eleven PSG writes: register addresses (low nibbles 0 4 8 11 1 5 12 9 3 7 10) and values equal the `.expect` log lines and the run's `--psg-log-out`; each read-back equals `value AND write-mask AND read-mask` of the register literal (the shape register `$01FA` reads 0, the policy of C75).
- Channel A period registers `$01F4 = 01` and `$01F0 = 23` combine to `$0123` = 291. Channel B amplitude 8 with the mode bit clear and the mixer value `3E` (tone B and noise B disabled): level B is 0 before sample 41 and 8 from sample 41 on (the write at tick 664; sample i stamped (i + 1) x 16 sees a write below the stamp) and `output.audio.changes` is 1. Level A is 0 in all 1047 delivered samples: the locked first-reload rule (4096 steps of 16 ticks, C77 and `psg.counter_model`) keeps tone A silent beyond the halt at tick 16752. No edge spacing is asserted anywhere.
- Sample count: `floor(16752 / 16) - floor(0 / 16)` = 1047 equals `output.audio.samples`, the raw file (4188 bytes = 1047 x 4) and the report.
- Result: all comparisons pass; no discrepancy found between the recomputation, the `.expect`, the scenario and the run.

## 3. Accepted caveats

- **Shared misreading.** The same author wrote the controller literal, the register literal, the derivations, the listings and the core. The blind second reads (controller 31 of 31 rows; PSG 90 of 120 vectors agree) are the same model family reading the same pages: a transcription cross-check, not independent evidence. Agreement between the pieces is not evidence about a console.
- **Tier 4 pin table.** The controller table is a LOW-confidence tier 4 literal from two community sources that are likely one lineage (C16); there is no schematic. The second reads are done (`docs/input/second-read-record.md`, `docs/psg/second-read-record-*.md`); none is pending. They do not raise a tier.
- **C67 unit-start granularity is a contract.** An event takes effect at the first CPU-unit boundary whose start tick is at or after its tick; a real console may differ by up to one instruction. A refinement changes goldens that carry input.
- **Repeatability only.** The sample-exact goldens, the digests, the consumer golden and the `.refpcm` golden are self-recorded: they show the same machine produces the same bytes, not that the bytes are right. The `.refpcm` golden is labelled "reference conversion v1, converter repeatability only", is a simple aliasing reference and sits outside the raw-audio evidence path.
- **`BUFFER_TOO_SMALL` is reachable through the public API** for audio and the write log now (the Phase 4 verification warning 3, which said it could be proven only through an internal seam, is closed): `psg.buffers.too-small-no-write`, `psg.buffers.log-too-small`, `run.buffers.psglog-overlap`.
- **Composite digest version 2** and the golden re-blesses (four classified diffs: composite v2 in plan 05-03, audio live in 05-06, noise and envelope in 05-07, and the runner key list with the `psglog` domain in 05-09; the pre-existing `diag-cpu-1`, `diag-input-pass` and `timeout-budget` goldens were unchanged by plan 05-11).
- **Sequential 12-wave cut and the counts against the D-25 guideline (A-08).** The guideline was about 365 test ids, at most 26 faults and about 22 controls; the phase added 956 expected test ids over both presets (about 478 per preset), 42 faults and 39 controls. The per-plan caps stayed binding; the guideline was exceeded.
- **Noise and envelope conventions are recorded, not measured:** noise first bits list the output after each shift, the envelope's first level change is 2 x EP steps after a restart (POL-PSG-02).
- **`psg.mix.truth-table` is polarity-invariant:** a flipped mixer sense does not fail that row; `PSG_MIXER_SENSE_INVERTED` is caught by `psg.mix.and-tone0` (registered under AUD-01).
- **Profile tag of `psg.mixer.port_dir_bits` (C73):** the field and the discrepancy now both say `unknown-default-chosen` (plan 05-12 aligned the discrepancy text; D-04 said `inference`). Behaviour is unchanged.
- **Phase 4 review deferrals IN-04, IN-06, IN-09, IN-10 and IN-11 stay open.**
- **Hosted cost.** Hosted rows read 45 to 109 s and 136 to 336 cpu-s since the Phase 4 close; last-5 medians 78 s and 231 cpu-s against the budgets (median 260 cpu-s with revisit above 280; 75 s with revisit above 90 s or any run above 120 s).
