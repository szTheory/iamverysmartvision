path — recompute the release-decision line with the values measured at this commit, and accept PKG-06 with the
three registry rows that name its mechanisms and the toy extension that keeps the derived set closed.

throwaway check, recorded, never in CI. The release-decision line is the milestone's third named disposition
(GUARDRAILS §7.4), and its own section says the line is recomputed rather than quoted.

`Complete`, three `release-posture` rows and thirteen toy lines.
