Coverage tags (checked against the vector's own data; a tag that its data does not prove fails `vector-tag-false`):

- `cycles:base` / `cycles:mod` (single-step vector; expect.cycles equals the leading integer of the row's `cycles_printed`, or that plus the `+k` of its one modifier); `cycles:n0` / `cycles:n1` (shift rows, printed `6/8`: kind=source vectors whose `expect.cycles` is 6 for N=0 / 8 for N=1); `cycles:taken` / `cycles:not-taken` (kind=policy POL-BR-01 vectors on rows whose `cycles_printed` is exactly `7/9`: expect.cycles equals the larger / smaller printed value).
- `addr:direct|indirect|autoinc|stack|immediate` (first decle MMM field, bits 5..3, = 0 | 1-3 | 4-5 | 6 | 7; for an `addr:sdbd` vector the MMM field of its second word); `addr:sdbd` (first program word is $0001; the row-label check uses the second word); `addr:r7-dest` (destination field = 7 and expect.r has key "7").

- `ebci:e<N>-true|false` (N decimal 0-15 equals the first decle AND $F; `true` when N is in the vector's `ebci` list, `false` when it is not).
- `policy:POL-<AREA>-<NN>` (counts toward that policy; a vector's `policy` key counts the same way).
