Row shape (fixed contract; plan 01-09's checker validates exactly these keys):
- Top level: `schema_version` (1), `source` (`SRC-gi-cp1600-um-1975`), `note`, `rows` (array).
- Row keys: `id` (unique, ASCII `^[A-Z][A-Z0-9@]*$`, normally the mnemonic, for example `MVI@`), `mnemonic`, `aliases` (array of strings), `group` (one of `register-register`, `single-register`, `register-shift`, `internal-control`, `jump`, `branch`, `direct-memory`, `indirect-memory`, `immediate`), `printed` (encoding exactly as printed, for example `0010 SSS DDD`), `pattern` (10 chars of `0`, `1`, `.`; index 0 is bit 9), `eq` (array like `["5:3=2:0"]`, bit ranges with bit 9 leftmost, for relational fields such as TSTR and CLRR), `refines` (id of the general row this specializes, or null), `also_encodes` (array of row ids sharing a leaf pattern by documented manual coincidence), `words` (decles in the instruction, number), `cycles_printed` (string exactly as printed, for example `7/9`), `cycle_modifiers` (array of strings, for example `+3 if MMM=6`), `sdbd` (`not-applicable`, `unsupported`, or `supported: <cycles as printed> (pdf 58)`), `status_bits` (string as printed, or `none`), `interruptible` (`yes`, `no` or `manual-silent`), `cite` (string containing `pdf <n>` and the section when printed), `note` (string, may be empty).
- Formatting: ASCII only, LF, 2-space indent, one key per line, rows in decle order of their pattern (lowest first, `.` read as 0).
</context>
