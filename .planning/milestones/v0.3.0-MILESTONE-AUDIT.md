## 5. What the milestone can and cannot claim

**One claim, `CL-0029`** — tier `deterministic-repeatability`, standing
`provisional`: the same core driven through a shared-object host reproduces the
runner's nine digest lines on a project cartridge. It compares the core with
itself, never an independent oracle. `CL-0002` remains the only `established`
claim.

**Nothing about real content or hardware.** No real EXEC, GROM or commercial
cartridge has been shown to boot, play, look or sound right; no claim reaches the
`measured-hardware` tier. The eight handoff rows (`D10`, `D11`, `H7-1`, `H7-2`,
`H8-3`, `H16-1`, `H16-2`, `H16-3`) are `evidence: none`, cited by no claim and by
no phase exit criterion, and are not re-asked.
