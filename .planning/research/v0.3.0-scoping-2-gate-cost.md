unchanged. `AUTONOMY.md` §4 allows it when the driver is otherwise stable — true: no phase

time and Actions minutes, not ratio: no checkpoint, no allowance credit.

## The checkpoint rule, `r` retired

At each phase boundary and at the close, by arithmetic from `ci-runs.tsv`:
last-five medians of `cpu_s`, `build_s`, `gate_s`; the token line; the phase's priced addition
charged against 21. Three verdicts:

- `discharged-within-allowance` — median `cpu_s` ≤ 361 + the phase's priced addition, cumulative ≤ 21.
- `reprice` — median moved more than the priced addition with an unchanged token line: a runner
  event, recorded, no lever invented.
- `lever-required` — cumulative would pass 21, or a phase overruns its line by >25 %.

`steps=` is an invariant: any commit where it is not `38/38` stops the milestone.
